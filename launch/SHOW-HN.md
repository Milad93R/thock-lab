**First comment**

I’m Milad, the maker of Thock Lab. It started as one key you could click, with a replaceable switch, cap and base. Desk mode takes that same switch and base sound and puts it under your physical keyboard. You can type in the pad or turn on Background to hear keys anywhere in the focused tab.

The sounds are synthesized in Web Audio. Desk mode pre-renders a bottom-out and a release for the selected setup, then varies pitch and gain slightly by key. Space gets a deeper sound than a letter key, and holding a key gives one press and one release.

Try switching between Deep Thock on walnut and Clicky on aluminum. I’d be interested in what you hear on fast rolls or on the longer keys. There’s no signup, and the code is on GitHub.
